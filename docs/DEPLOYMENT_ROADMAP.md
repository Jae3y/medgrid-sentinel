# Deployment Roadmap: what is standing, running and paid for in 6–18 months

| Months | What gets built, installed or signed | By whom | Exit gate (evidence) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1–3 Validate** | MoU with one Maiduguri-class referral hospital; circuit-level meters on Tier-1/2/3 boards and the generator; fuel log digitised; Sentinel runs in **shadow mode** (forecasts, governor recommendations and SMS alerts, no actuation); on-site irradiance sensor | Sentinel + hospital works dept. + local electrical contractor | Daily-energy prediction error ≤ ±15%; tier map signed by the works engineer; measured generator fuel curve |
| **4–6 Engineer & finance** | Sizing re-run on measured data; single-line design to IEC 62446 / 62109 / 62619 equipment and IEC 60364-7-710 (medical locations) practice; NEMSA inspection plan; EaaS term sheet (fee below verified savings) with a licensed EPC/ESCO; insurance | Licensed EPC/ESCO, Sentinel, hospital management | Signed EaaS/PPA; two EPC quotes within ±20% of model CapEx |
| **7–12 Pilot** | Install PV + LFP + PCS; Sentinel controller integrated through the inverter/generator controllers' certified interfaces; SMS/USSD alerts to the duty engineer; monthly measured-vs-predicted report | EPC (install), Sentinel (controls, reporting), hospital (operations) | 3 months of operation with no Tier-1 loss attributable to the system; measured diesel saving ≥ 75% of predicted |
| **13–18 Replicate** | Fleet screen picks sites 2–3 (Kano, Abuja); a standard design package reduces engineering time; second ESCO partner | Sentinel + ESCO partners | Two additional signed sites; per-site cost tracking against the fleet-cost hypothesis |

## Dependencies and risks by phase
- **1–3:** hospital access and data permission → the hospital owns its data, and no patient data is ever collected.
- **4–6:** EPC availability and FX → the quote gate; FX sensitivity in the model.
- **7–12:** security of assets → on-campus siting and insurance; commissioning schedule.
- **13–18:** collections track record from site 1 → used to unlock DFI or concessional capital.

## Hackathon pathway fit
POC (this sprint) → POC-to-MVP (2027 Global Energy Summit: show shadow-mode data) → MVP-to-Pilot (Q3 2027 Cohort: first installation).
