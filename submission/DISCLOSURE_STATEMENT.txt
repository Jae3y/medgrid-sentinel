2. WHAT WAS BUILT DURING THE SPRINT (all visible, timestamped, in git history)
Day 1 (30 Oct, first version, built with ZCode): a deterministic 15-minute microgrid simulation engine (PV, battery, generator, grid, power-quality events, tiered dispatch), Next.js product UI, financial/forecast/sizing modules, documentation and first submission artifacts.
Day 2 (31 Oct, rebuild, built with Claude): engine v2 — measured NASA POWER weather, diesel tank and resupply chain, four dispatch strategies on identical hardware, the Sentinel fuel-runway governor / clinical ride-through reserve / generator cycling, generator transfer-break physics, the Brief #13 Maiduguri 39-day line-outage replay, Monte Carlo resilience with published generator reliability data, a solar forecaster scored on held-out data, full-year 20-year economics with EaaS provider returns, sizing optimizer, fleet screen, SMS/USSD alert channel, offline static export; new UI (Line Outage, Resilience Lab, Fleet & Scale, rebuilt Financials/Brief), 101 automated tests, end-to-end browser walkthrough, regenerated deck, one-page summary, documentation and video scripts.

3. AI TOOLS USED (permitted under Handbook s13/s17 with disclosure)
- ZCode (GLM-based coding agent) — 30 Oct: generated the first version of code, tests, documents and artifacts from the team's written brief.
- Claude Code with Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) — 31 Oct: research assistance, engineering re-design, code, tests, documentation and artifact generation, under the team's direction and review.
- AI was used as a development instrument. Engineering judgements, results and claims were checked against computed outputs and primary sources; every number in the deck, summary and scripts is generated by the engine (results/demo-results.json, `npm run results`).
- No AI-generated video, voice, imagery or fabricated evidence. The demo and pitch videos are recorded by the team. Product screenshots are real captures of the running application.
- No confidential, partner or personal information was entered into any AI tool.
- The product itself contains no generative AI: dispatch is deterministic; the forecaster is a least-squares model trained on public data.
