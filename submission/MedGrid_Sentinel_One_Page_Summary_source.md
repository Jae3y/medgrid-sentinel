## Community & target user - who, and who pays

Referral hospitals in cities whose transmission line is repeatedly cut - instantiated on Maiduguri, Borno State (Gombe-Damaturu-Maiduguri 330 kV line down 22 Sep - 31 Oct 2024; ~2 months in 2021). User: the works engineer and duty manager. Beneficiaries: ICU, theatre, maternity, cold-chain and emergency patients (250 beds = 91,250 bed-days/yr). Payer: hospital management, or an energy-service company (ESCO) repaid from diesel no longer bought.

## The problem - what fails today, and why

Backup is sized in kW, but a line outage is an energy-logistics crisis: diesel need rises ~4x (3,360 L/week in a normal year, simulated) while deliveries and budgets stay flat. In Dec 2022 two Maiduguri hospitals sat dark for days with working generators and empty tanks (ICIR). Only 50% of hospitals in sub-Saharan Africa report reliable electricity (WHO et al., 2023). Root cause: no one decides, in advance and quantitatively, which loads the remaining litres protect.

## Proposed solution - what we built and how it works

A hospital-microgrid digital twin (15-min steps, 16 tiered circuits, PV, LFP storage, generator, diesel tank and resupply chain) with a predictive controller: (1) a fuel-runway governor that compares diesel + battery + forecast solar with each care tier's energy need until the next expected delivery and defers the least critical loads first; (2) a clinical ride-through reserve so a failed generator never darkens the ICU; (3) efficient generator cycling. Offline web app + SMS/USSD alerts.

## Estimated cost & who pays - can the lowest-income users afford it?

Reference design (optimizer-selected) 600 kWp PV + 600 kWh LFP + 510 kW PCS + Sentinel edge: $704,415 (N940.4M). Savings N311.2M/yr -> payback 3.3 yr, IRR 30%, 20-yr NPV N553.2M at 18%. Who pays: under EaaS the ESCO owns and maintains; the hospital pays N20.7M/month (80% of measured savings), zero CapEx, and keeps 20% from month one (provider IRR 24%). Energy cost per bed-day falls N8,940 -> N8,258; nothing is passed to patients. No grant assumed.

## 6-18-month deployment path - what gets built, by whom

M1-3 metering and circuit audit at one Maiduguri-class hospital; Sentinel in shadow mode on real data. M4-6 engineering design (IEC 62446/62619/62109 equipment, NEMSA inspection) and EaaS term sheet with a licensed installer. M7-12 install; Sentinel dispatches; SMS alerts live; measured-vs-predicted reported monthly. M13-18 sites 2-3 (Kano, Abuja).

## How it scales - what changes in a second community or country

Only configuration: measured weather (same NASA POWER pipeline), load profile, tariff, feeder regime, standards. Fleet screen of 10 site-years (250- and 80-bed hospitals): payback 2.9-4.9 yr in Abuja, Kano, Maiduguri, Port Harcourt; 5.9-6.2 yr in Lagos (better grid, weaker sun) - the tool says where it pays. Fleet procurement + standard design: assumed 6% cost drop per doubling (hypothesis).

## Biggest risk & how we will test it early

Modelled loads and generator condition may differ from the real hospital. Test: 3 months of shadow-mode operation on circuit-level metering before any capital; proceed only if daily-energy prediction error is within +/-15%. Secondary: theft/vandalism of panels - on-campus siting and insurance priced into the EaaS fee.
