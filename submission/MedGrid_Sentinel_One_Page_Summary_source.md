**MEDGRID SENTINEL** — Intelligent Hospital Microgrids · Predictive Energy Resilience · Uninterrupted Critical Care

Anneru IAS Global Energy Hackathon 2026 (Oct 30 – Nov 1) · **Brief #13 — Power for a city the grid can't protect** · **Track 2** Digital Solutions & AI · **Setting B** Non-residential (healthcare) · Team: Jackson Sixtus Abetianbe (solo entry - permitted per Handbook s8)

**Community & target user.** Urban Nigerian hospitals on feeders that fail with the national grid (250-bed reference hospital, illustrative). User: facility/works engineer and duty manager. Payer: the hospital, or an energy-service provider under Energy-as-a-Service — the structure of the real NSIA-LUTH cancer-centre microgrid (300 kWp/1.1 MWh, 2025).

**The problem.** Nigeria's grid failed 12+ times in 2024 (industry counts; NERC official count lower). Each collapse drops hospitals onto diesel at ≈7× grid cost (≈₦1,560/L vs ≈₦210/kWh) and threatens theatres, ICU and the vaccine cold chain. Solar+storage is bought blind: no tool shows whether a design holds critical loads through a real outage, or what each avoided diesel litre costs. Root cause: decision quality, not hardware.

**Proposed solution.** A deterministic hospital-microgrid digital twin: a 15-minute-step simulation of PV, battery, grid and genset dispatching around tiered clinical loads (life-critical / clinical-priority / deferrable), with outage islanding and honest deficit reporting — plus a ten-scenario stress lab, an EaaS financial engine, a dual-metric sizing optimizer and explainable predictive intelligence. No black box touches dispatch; the core runs offline with no external APIs.

**Proof of Concept for the 48 hours.** The full application built during the sprint: deterministic engine (power balance verified every step), 58 unit tests with hand-calculated values, production build verified, results reproducible via `npm run results`.

**Estimated cost & who pays.** Reference CapEx ≈ **₦760.4M ($491k)**: payback **7.9 yr**, IRR 4.6%, NPV negative at an 18% commercial discount rate — stated plainly. EaaS fee ≈ ₦161M/yr vs ₦112M/yr operating savings: closes below a computed **4.6% financing rate** — exactly the gap REA-type/DFI programmes exist to fill; service cost ≈ **₦1,764/bed-day**. No grant assumed anywhere in the model.

**6–18-month deployment path.** M1–3 metering-first pilot on one real feeder → M4–6 engineering design, permits, financing & O&M agreements with an energy-service partner → M7–12 pilot deployed with measured-vs-predicted evidence → M13–18 replicate to 3–5 facilities. Installation is by licensed partners — Sentinel is decision support, never a control system.

**How it scales.** One facility = one configuration object (loads, tariffs, outage pattern, climate); the rural-clinic profile runs on the same engine today. The sizing optimizer proves designs must match outage timing: daytime outages → PV-first (no battery optimal); evening outages → storage-heavy. Country adaptation = parameter changes, not code changes.

**Biggest risk & how we test it.** Simulated facility data could mislead sizing. Mitigation is structural: every input labelled, the engine accepts measured AMI data unchanged, and the roadmap is metering-first — month 1 delivers the feeder's real outage pattern before any CapEx decision.
