# Research Limitations

1. **Full Brief #13 text not available in the workspace.** Built against Appendix B title and metric; reconciliation checklist prepared.
2. **No measured load data for a Maiduguri hospital** was publicly available; the reference profile is illustrative and labelled everywhere.
3. **Generator reliability** statistics are from U.S. defence and nuclear fleets and a Hong Kong data set (NREL report). No published Nigerian hospital generator failure data was found; the three classes bracket the plausible range.
4. **Outage duration distribution** for Nigerian feeders: the World Bank Enterprise Survey gives frequency (32.8 per month, 2014); durations reported in secondary sources conflict, so median 4 h is an assumption with sensitivity.
5. **Diesel prices** come from aggregators (Legit.ng, Finelib) rather than NBS for October 2026; the NBS figure quoted by one outlet (₦3,277/L) could not be dated and was not used.
6. **Storage and PV installed costs in Nigeria** lack a primary public benchmark; we scale BNEF global turnkey prices by ~2.2× for import, logistics and security premia, and test ±30%.
7. **NSIA-LUTH project performance** is projected by the developer (61.5% renewable, −88.8% diesel), not independently measured.
8. **Maiduguri residual supply** during the 2024 outage (from the emergency gas plant) to individual hospital feeders is undocumented; it is modelled with a slider.
