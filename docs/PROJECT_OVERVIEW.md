# Project Overview

**MedGrid Sentinel:** fuel-aware hospital microgrids for cities the grid can't protect.

- **Brief #13** "Power for a city the grid can't protect" · **Track 2** Digital Solutions & AI · **Setting B** Non-residential (healthcare) · solo entry: Jackson Sixtus Abetianbe.
- **Metric (verbatim):** "Hours of critical service maintained during a line outage, and diesel litres avoided."

## In one paragraph
In a long transmission-line outage, a hospital's diesel need roughly quadruples while deliveries and budgets do not. Hospitals go dark with working generators and empty tanks, as two Maiduguri hospitals did in December 2022. MedGrid Sentinel is a hospital-microgrid digital twin with a predictive controller. A fuel-runway governor schedules care tiers against the next delivery; a clinical ride-through reserve and efficient generator cycling complete it. We replayed the real 39-day Maiduguri line outage (Sep–Oct 2024) on NASA POWER measured weather with four controllers and identical fuel. Critical-service hours go from 449 to 937 on 2,841 fewer litres, and the 14-day probability of keeping life-critical circuits powered goes from 0% to 78%. CapEx is $704k with a 3.3-yr payback, or zero hospital CapEx under energy-as-a-service.
